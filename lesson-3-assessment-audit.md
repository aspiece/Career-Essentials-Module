# Lesson 3 Assessment Audit

**Scope:** Draft assessment in `lesson-3-assessment.md`, aligned to Lesson 3: *Trust & Goal Setting at Work*.

**Decision:** Ready for instructor review. The assessment is a local draft and has not been published or built in an external quiz platform.

## Target-to-item alignment

| Learning target | Evidence in the lesson | Assessment evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identify behaviors that build or weaken workplace trust. | Trust video/text alternative, career-field comparison, and checks for understanding in Step 3.2. | Questions 1 and 3 ask learners to recognize early communication and a trust-building response. | Pass |
| Explain how accountability supports a team. | Definition, modeled response, self-check, and accountability-plan prompt in Steps 3.2 and 3.4. | Question 2 asks learners to identify ownership and a next step; the constructed response requires an accountability plan. | Pass |
| Create a SMART goal for a workplace skill or task. | SMART video/text alternative, vocabulary, career-field comparison, practice fields, self-check, and final-response preparation in Steps 3.3–3.5. | Questions 4–9 assess SMART components and the constructed response requires transfer to a workplace goal. | Pass |

## Reading level and clarity

The items use short sentences, familiar workplace situations, and terms that are defined in the lesson. The assessment is appropriate for an 11th-grade CTE audience while remaining accessible to a broad range of readers. The short, direct wording may produce a lower automated readability score than the grade band; that reflects test-item format, not reduced cognitive demand.

Terms such as *trust*, *accountability*, *specific*, *measurable*, *achievable*, *relevant*, and *time-bound* are all introduced, modeled, and practiced before assessment.

## Accessibility and delivery checks

- Put the constructed response first, followed by the nine selected-response items.
- Make the goal-and-plan response required and keep it for instructor scoring.
- Use one question per screen or clearly separated sections if the course platform supports it.
- Preserve the lesson's transcript-based video alternatives and vocabulary pop-ups; no video viewing is required to access the tested ideas.
- Before creating a visual quiz, select or confirm a color treatment. The assessment-builder workflow does not apply color without the instructor's choice.
