## Instructor scoring for the opening response

The paragraph response is manually graded out of 2 points. Google Forms provides a points field and feedback for paragraphs, but no private rubric field. Keep this guide instructor-facing and apply it when reviewing responses; the rubric has not been exposed as student answer feedback.

Score the response as a whole. Evaluate the quality of the goal and plan rather than specialized technical knowledge from the student's career field.

| Score | Instructor guidance |
|---:|---|
| 2 | The response names a workplace-relevant action, a measure or evidence of progress, a deadline or check-in date, and a realistic accountability plan that identifies a person, team, check-in, or evidence of follow-through. |
| 1 | The response includes a goal but misses one essential element, or it gives a vague measure, deadline, or accountability plan. |
| 0 | The response is incomplete, not connected to a workplace action, or does not provide enough information to evaluate a goal and follow-through plan. |

Immediate grade release shows the automatically scored portion before the instructor assigns the paragraph score. The final total requires instructor grading.
