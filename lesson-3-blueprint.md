# Lesson 3: Trust & Goal Setting at Work

## Purpose

Students connect the Rock Wall experience to workplace trust, accountability, and SMART goal setting.

## Learning targets

- I can identify actions that build workplace trust.
- I can explain how accountability supports a team.
- I can create a SMART goal and accountability plan for my work environment.

## Sequence

| Step | Purpose | Learner action | Estimated time |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 3.1 | Orient | Review outcomes, standards, pacing, and vocabulary. | 4 min |
| 3.2 | Build knowledge | Watch a short trust video or read its text alternative; select a career field; compare matched trust-building and trust-weakening actions; complete three checks. | 11 min |
| 3.3 | Model application | Watch a short SMART-goals video or read its text alternative; use the selected career field to compare a vague intention with a SMART goal; complete three checks. | 11 min |
| 3.4 | Transfer | Create a trust-building action, SMART goal, and accountability plan. | 15 min |
| 3.5 | Assess | Prepare a final goal and accountability plan for the course quiz. | 8 min |

## Make-up assignment treatment

At the beginning of Step 3.1, students who missed the Rock Wall experience are directed to complete the Rock Wall Make-Up Assignment (`https://forms.gle/jUe2mpQCvoJbDPLx6`) before continuing through every Lesson 3 step.

## National career-readiness alignment

- Advance CTE Career Ready Practice: Act as a responsible and contributing citizen and employee.
- NACE Professionalism Competency: Demonstrate personal accountability and effective work habits.

## Assessment plan

The planned course quiz will assess trust-building actions, accountability, and the SMART framework. The final constructed response will ask students to submit one workplace-relevant SMART goal with an accountability plan.
