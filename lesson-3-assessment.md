# Lesson 3 Assessment: Trust & Goal Setting at Work

**Total: 20 points**  
**Format: 10 questions worth 2 points each**

## Student assessment

Begin with the final workplace goal. Then choose the best answer for Questions 1–9.

You may take this quiz up to 3 times. Contact your instructor to request a second attempt.

### Final workplace goal and accountability plan (2 points)

Using the career field you selected in Lesson 3, write one workplace SMART goal and an accountability plan. Your response must name a specific work action, show how you will measure progress, include a deadline or check-in date, and explain how you will show follow-through to a person or team.

### 1. Which response best builds trust when work may be late? (2 points)

A. Wait for the supervisor's request.  
B. Report the delay and next step.  
C. Keep the delay private for now.  
D. Ask another worker to explain it.  

### 2. Which action shows accountability? (2 points)

A. Name the error and next step.  
B. Name another person as cause.  
C. Wait for a manager to ask.  
D. Keep the issue to yourself.  

### 3. When should a worker ask for help with a barrier? (2 points)

A. Before the deadline is at risk.  
B. After the deadline has passed.  
C. Only when a manager notices.  
D. When no work is left to do.  

### 4. Which goal is most specific? (2 points)

A. Improve my customer service.  
B. Practice better communication.  
C. Use the greeting script daily.  
D. Try to be more professional.  

### 5. Which detail makes a goal measurable? (2 points)

A. Use a checklist for five shifts.  
B. Use a checklist with extra care.  
C. Use a checklist whenever possible.  
D. Use a checklist to improve work.  

### 6. Which goal is achievable with the available time and support? (2 points)

You are learning a stock-count routine. You have 15 minutes to practice on Tuesday and Thursday, a checklist, and a trainer available on Tuesday. Each practice takes 10 minutes.

A. Complete three stock-count practices during each 15-minute session.  
B. Practice for 10 minutes with the trainer on Tuesday and Thursday.  
C. Practice for 10 minutes with the trainer Tuesday and the checklist Thursday.  
D. Practice for 10 minutes every weekday using the stock-count checklist.  

### 7. Which goal is most relevant to the repair team's current need? (2 points)

A repair team loses time looking for shared tools before each job. The team wants to reduce that delay.

A. Record the repairs completed at the end of each shift.  
B. Check shared tools against their location list before each shift.  
C. Review the customer greeting script before the first appointment.  
D. Sort delivery receipts by date at the end of each shift.  

### 8. Which goal is most time-bound? (2 points)

A. During next week, log five repairs.  
B. Before future shifts, log repairs.  
C. At some point, log five repairs.  
D. Whenever ready, log five repairs.  

### 9. Which goal meets all five SMART criteria in this situation? (2 points)

Your team needs a stocked glove shelf each day. You have five shifts through Friday, five minutes per shift, the needed supplies and checklist, and a lead to demonstrate the three-minute restocking task.

A. Through Friday, I will improve the glove shelf each shift, record five checks, and show my log to my lead on Friday.  
B. I will restock the glove shelf whenever I can, record each completed check, and show my log to my lead when I feel ready.  
C. Through Friday, I will restock the glove shelf three times each shift, record all checks, and show my log to my lead on Friday.  
D. Through Friday, I will restock the glove shelf each shift, record all five checks, and show my log to my lead on Friday.

### Final workplace goal and accountability-plan scoring (2 points)

Score the response as a whole. Evaluate the quality of the goal and plan rather than specialized technical knowledge from the student's career field. The four scored elements remain: a workplace-relevant action, a measure or evidence of progress, a deadline or check-in date, and a realistic accountability plan showing follow-through to a person or team. Students do not need to label the SMART letters or submit separate achievable/relevant explanations. Their planning notes support those judgments; they are not additional graded requirements.

Apply the 0-point rule first. If it does not apply, award 2 points when all four elements are clear and usable; otherwise award 1 point.

| Score | Instructor guidance |
|---:|---|
| 2 | All four elements are clear and usable: a workplace-relevant action, a measure or evidence of progress, a deadline or check-in date, and a realistic accountability plan showing how follow-through will be shared with a person or team. |
| 1 | The response gives a recognizable workplace goal and usable information for at least one of the four scored elements, but one or more elements are missing, vague, or unrealistic. It does not meet all four requirements for 2 points. |
| 0 | The response is blank, off-topic, gives no recognizable workplace goal, or provides no usable information for any of the four scored elements. A broad wish such as wanting to do better, without a task, evidence, timing, or follow-through, earns 0 points. |

#### Short scoring anchors

- **2 points:** “By Friday, I will restock the glove shelf at the start of all five shifts, record each completed check, and show my five-entry log to my lead on Friday.” All four scored elements are present.
- **1 point:** “I will restock the glove shelf at the start of each shift, record each completed check, and show my log to my lead.” The action, evidence, and accountability are usable, but there is no deadline or bounded review point.
- **0 points:** “I want to do better at work.” No task or other scored element is usable.
