# Lesson 3 Assessment: Trust & Goal Setting at Work

**Total: 20 points**  
**Format: 10 questions worth 2 points each**

## Student assessment

Begin with the final workplace goal. Then choose the best answer for Questions 1–9.

You may take this quiz up to 3 times. Contact your instructor to request a second attempt.

### Final workplace goal and accountability plan (2 points)

Using the career field you selected in Lesson 3, write one workplace SMART goal and an accountability plan. Your response must name a specific work action, show how you will measure progress, include a deadline or check-in date, and explain how you will show follow-through to a person or team.

### 1. Which response best builds trust when work may be late? (2 points)

A. Wait for the supervisor's request.  
B. Report the delay and next step.  
C. Keep the delay private for now.  
D. Ask another worker to explain it.  

### 2. Which action shows accountability? (2 points)

A. Name the error and next step.  
B. Name another person as cause.  
C. Wait for a manager to ask.  
D. Keep the issue to yourself.  

### 3. When should a worker ask for help with a barrier? (2 points)

A. Before the deadline is at risk.  
B. After the deadline has passed.  
C. Only when a manager notices.  
D. When no work is left to do.  

### 4. Which goal is most specific? (2 points)

A. Improve my customer service.  
B. Practice better communication.  
C. Use the greeting script daily.  
D. Try to be more professional.  

### 5. Which detail makes a goal measurable? (2 points)

A. Use a checklist for five shifts.  
B. Use a checklist with extra care.  
C. Use a checklist whenever possible.  
D. Use a checklist to improve work.  

### 6. Which goal is achievable? (2 points)

A. Practice with a mentor weekly.  
B. Master every task by Friday.  
C. Finish a month of work today.  
D. Complete all training at once.  

### 7. Which goal is most relevant to a repair team? (2 points)

A. Organize tools for the repair team.  
B. Learn a hobby during each shift.  
C. Practice a sport before the shift.  
D. Plan weekend activities at work.  

### 8. Which goal is most time-bound? (2 points)

A. During next week, log five repairs.  
B. Before future shifts, log repairs.  
C. At some point, log five repairs.  
D. Whenever ready, log five repairs.  

### 9. Which goal includes all parts of SMART? (2 points)

A. I will improve stocking this week and ask my lead for help as needed.  
B. I will restock gloves daily through Friday and show my log to my lead.  
C. I will work on stocking this week and use extra effort whenever possible.  
D. I will improve supplies this week and ask coworkers for help as needed.  

## Instructor answer key and scoring guidance

| Question | Correct answer | Points | Rationale |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | B | 2 | An early update names the problem and gives the team a next step. |
| 2 | A | 2 | Accountability means owning the issue and following through on a response. |
| 3 | A | 2 | Early communication gives the team time to adjust before work is late. |
| 4 | C | 2 | It names the exact action to perform. |
| 5 | A | 2 | Five shifts gives the worker evidence they can count or check. |
| 6 | A | 2 | A weekly practice routine with a mentor is realistic with support. |
| 7 | A | 2 | Organizing tools supports the repair team's work. |
| 8 | A | 2 | “During next week” provides a clear time frame. |
| 9 | B | 2 | It states an action, a measure, a deadline, and a way to show follow-through. |

### Final workplace goal and accountability-plan scoring (2 points)

Score the response as a whole. Evaluate the quality of the goal and plan rather than specialized technical knowledge from the student's career field.

| Score | Instructor guidance |
|---:|---|
| 2 | The response names a workplace-relevant action, a measure or evidence of progress, a deadline or check-in date, and a realistic accountability plan that identifies a person, team, check-in, or evidence of follow-through. |
| 1 | The response includes a goal but misses one essential element, or it gives a vague measure, deadline, or accountability plan. |
| 0 | The response is incomplete, not connected to a workplace action, or does not provide enough information to evaluate a goal and follow-through plan. |
