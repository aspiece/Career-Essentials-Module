## Recommended revision for Question 6

Replace the current stem and choices with the following, retaining a 2-point score.

**Which message is concise and concrete?**

- A. Handle gloves in Cabinet B by 2.
- B. Restock supplies in Cabinet B by 2.
- C. Restock gloves in Cabinet B soon.
- D. Restock gloves in Cabinet B by 2. **(correct)**

This version makes the expected action, item, location, and exact deadline unambiguously strongest while preserving the lesson construct. The choices also remain within four characters in length.
