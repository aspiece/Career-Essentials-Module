# Lesson 2: Workplace Communication — The 7 Cs

## Purpose

Students use the 7 Cs to make a workplace message understandable, actionable, and respectful.

## Source boundaries

- Preserve: The 7 Cs framework and the selected Mind Tools video.
- Reorganize: The video concepts into a five-step independent lesson.
- Rewrite: Definitions in student-friendly language.
- Proposed additions: All workplace scenarios, checks, and the career-program application.
- Gaps: Course quiz location and grading rules are not yet supplied.

## Learning targets

- I can identify the 7 Cs in workplace messages.
- I can explain how an unclear message can affect work.
- I can revise a workplace message to make it clear, complete, and courteous.

## Sequence

| Step | Purpose | Learner action | Source locator | Program treatment | Estimated time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 2.1 | Orient | Read targets and challenge. | Proposed addition | Program-neutral | 5 min |
| 2.2 | Build knowledge | Watch video; answer three checks. | Selected video | Program-neutral | 10 min |

| 2.4 | Transfer | Use the selected career field to revise its vague workplace message, self-check the draft, compare it with an exemplar, and interactively identify four emphasized 7 Cs. | Proposed addition | Learner-selected workplace scenario | 15 min |

## Assessment

The website provides practice feedback. The course quiz assessment contains 10 questions worth 2 points each (20 points total): nine selected-response items and one final-message response. The final message is scored for audience, task, actionable details, and professional communication.

## National career-readiness alignment

- Advance CTE, National Career Clusters Framework: Career Ready Practice, “Communicate clearly, effectively, and within reason.”
- National Association of Colleges and Employers: Communication competency, including clear, organized communication and appropriate questions for supervisors and others.

## Master instructional-design audit

| Review lane | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Objective alignment | Students needed repeated practice before independent writing. | Added three checks in Step 2.2 and three checks in Step 2.3 before the application task. |
| Assessment construction | Original options varied in length and each check had only two items. | Each check now has three four-option items; choices within an item use similar character length and correct answers vary by position. |
| Authentic transfer | Students needed a concrete model connected to their interests. | Students select a career field, improve its vague message, compare it with a matched exemplar, and interactively identify four 7 Cs. |
| Accessibility | Video required a text equivalent. | Added a concise instructional text alternative for the video and retained the direct video link. |
| Standards | National career-readiness connection was not visible to students. | Added Advance CTE and NACE communication alignment in the lesson overview. |

## Make-up assignment treatment

At the beginning of Step 2.1, students who missed Base Camp are directed to complete the Effective Communication Make-Up Assignment (`https://forms.gle/WY7KYPsiuiDaqauaA`) before continuing through every Lesson 2 step. The notice states that the make-up assignment does not replace Lesson 2.

## Accessibility and UDL plan

Semantic headings, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, direct video link, readable contrast, reflow-friendly grids, student-controlled pacing, and written as well as video representation.

## Completion directions

Students use their completed planning and revision work to answer the course quiz, then enter their polished workplace message in the final-message question for scoring.
