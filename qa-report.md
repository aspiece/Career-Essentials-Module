## Assessment reading level and rigor review

| Area | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Reading accessibility | Pass | The student assessment has an estimated Flesch–Kincaid grade of 3.65. Short stems and answer choices support independent access; the workplace concepts remain appropriate for grades 11–12. |
| Cognitive rigor | Pass | Questions 1–9 require students to apply the 7 Cs to likely workplace outcomes, audience, task specificity, completeness, tone, concision, correctness, coherence, and revision. Question 10 requires transfer to a career-specific workplace message. |
| Distractor construction | Pass | Each selected-response item has four plausible, parallel choices. Correct answers are evenly distributed across positions A, C, and D. |
